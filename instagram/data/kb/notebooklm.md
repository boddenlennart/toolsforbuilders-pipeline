## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Competitive Content Analysis
1. Create a new notebook titled "[Competitor] Analysis"
2. Upload 10-15 of competitor's best-performing posts/articles/videos (via URL)
3. Ask NotebookLM: "What themes appear most frequently across these sources?"
4. Ask: "What gaps or contradictions exist in their content?"
5. Use gaps as your unique content angles
Result: Competitive gaps identified in 15 minutes, unique positioning discovered

### Workflow: Book Research → Content Library
1. Upload your research PDFs, notes, and reference articles (up to 50 sources)
2. Ask: "Generate a study guide covering all key concepts"
3. Ask: "What are the most quotable insights for social content?"
4. Export quotes/insights to your content database
5. Generate an Audio Overview for commute listening
Result: Research library you can query anytime + podcast for passive review

### Workflow: Course Content from Expertise
1. Upload your existing content: blog posts, transcripts, notes
2. Ask: "Organize these into a logical curriculum structure"
3. For each module, ask: "What exercises would reinforce this concept?"
4. Generate Video Overview for each module preview
5. Use as basis for course outline
Result: Course structure from existing content, 2-3 hours instead of weeks

### Workflow: Podcast-Style Content Creation
1. Upload 3-5 articles on a topic you want to discuss
2. Generate Audio Overview — creates a podcast-style discussion
3. Listen and note the most interesting angles
4. Use angles as your own content hooks
5. The AI hosts debate perspectives you might have missed
Result: Content angles you wouldn't have found alone, 10-minute process
