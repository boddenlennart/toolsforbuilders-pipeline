## Core Facts (verified)
- free_tier: 300 minutes/month transcription, 30 minutes max per conversation, 3 file imports total (not per month)
- paid_tier: Pro $16.99/mo (annual), Business $30/mo (annual), 1,200-6,000 min/mo depending on tier
- free_tier_model: Full transcription quality, limited usage
- context_window: N/A — processes audio/video files and live meetings
- best_for: Meeting transcription, Zoom/Google Meet integration, searchable conversation history
- not_good_for: Long meetings on free tier (30 min limit), high-volume transcription needs, offline use
- solopreneur_score: 78/100 — Great for occasional meetings, free tier hits limits quickly

## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Client Call Documentation
1. Connect Otter to Zoom/Google Meet
2. Otter auto-joins and transcribes your calls
3. After call: Review AI-generated summary
4. Export action items to your task manager
Result: Every client call documented with searchable transcript

### Workflow: Content Ideas from Conversations
1. Record brainstorming sessions or podcast interviews
2. Otter transcribes and identifies speakers
3. Search transcripts for specific topics mentioned
4. Export quotes for content repurposing
Result: Conversations become searchable content goldmine

### Workflow: Meeting Minutes Automation
1. Otter joins team meetings automatically
2. AI generates summary with key points
3. Share summary link with attendees
4. Action items assigned directly from transcript
Result: No more manual meeting notes
