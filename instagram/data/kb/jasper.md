## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Brand Voice Setup (worth it IF you have budget)
1. Upload examples of your best content
2. Jasper analyzes and creates brand voice profile
3. All future content matches your voice
4. Set tone, style, and vocabulary guidelines
5. Team members maintain consistency
Result: Consistent brand voice at scale

### Workflow: Marketing Campaign Creation
1. Enter campaign brief and goals
2. Jasper generates coordinated assets:
   - Ad copy variations
   - Landing page content
   - Email sequences
   - Social posts
3. Edit and export to platforms
Result: Full campaign content in one session

### Workflow: Long-Form Content
1. Use document editor with AI assist
2. Write outline, AI expands sections
3. Boss Mode for longer generation
4. Built-in plagiarism checker
5. Export polished content
Result: Blog posts and articles faster

### Better alternative for most solopreneurs:
- Claude Pro ($20/mo) + Canva ($15/mo) = $35/mo
- Gets you better AI + design tools
- Save $4-14/mo vs Jasper Creator
