## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Free Image Generation Pipeline
1. Open AI Studio (aistudio.google.com) for highest free limits (500-1,000/day)
2. Use Imagen 3 model for generation
3. Prompt with style references: "Product photo style, [your product], white background, studio lighting"
4. Generate 3-5 variations, download best ones
5. Edit in Canva if needed
Result: Professional product/social images at $0 cost

### Workflow: YouTube Research to Content
1. In Gemini, paste a YouTube link of competitor content
2. Ask: "Summarize key points, identify gaps, suggest angles they missed"
3. Use the gaps as your content hooks
4. Draft your take on the topic
Result: Competitive research in 5 minutes, unique angles identified

### Workflow: Deep Research for Blog Posts
1. Use Gemini Deep Research feature
2. Enter topic: "Latest trends in [your niche] with specific data points"
3. Gemini searches multiple sources, compiles with citations
4. Export to Google Docs, refine into your voice
5. Cross-reference claims before publishing
Result: Research-backed blog draft in 15 minutes

### Workflow: Daily Social Content with Free Limits
1. Morning: Generate 3-5 image options in AI Studio (uses ~5 of your 500 daily)
2. Midday: Use Gemini chat for caption drafts (uses ~3 of your 1,500 daily requests)
3. Afternoon: Research tomorrow's topic (uses ~5 requests)
4. Track usage in AI Studio dashboard
Result: Full content day without touching paid limits
