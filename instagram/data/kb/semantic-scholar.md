## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Free Research Discovery
1. Go to semanticscholar.org (no login needed)
2. Search your topic
3. AI shows key papers with relevance ranking
4. Click "TLDR" for AI summary of each paper
5. Follow citation networks to find more
Result: Deep research without spending anything

### Workflow: Stay Current in Your Niche
1. Create free account
2. Set up Research Feed for your topics
3. AI learns what papers you care about
4. Get personalized recommendations
5. Review weekly for new relevant research
Result: Always up-to-date on your niche

### Workflow: Extract Visual Data
1. Find paper with relevant charts/data
2. AI extracts figures and tables automatically
3. View data visualizations without reading full paper
4. Screenshot or reference in your content
5. Link to original source
Result: Data-rich content from academic sources

### Workflow: Build Citation Networks
1. Start with key paper in your field
2. See "Cited By" and "References"
3. Map influential papers and authors
4. Identify foundational vs. recent research
5. Build comprehensive understanding
Result: Expert-level knowledge of your topic
