## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Pitch Deck from Idea
1. Open Gamma, click "Generate presentation"
2. Enter prompt: "Pitch deck for [your business] targeting [audience]"
3. AI generates 10-12 slide structure with content
4. Edit text, swap images as needed
5. Export as PDF or share link
Result: Professional pitch deck in under 10 minutes

### Workflow: Blog Post to Presentation
1. Paste your blog post into Gamma
2. AI extracts key points and structures slides
3. Each section becomes a visual slide
4. Add your branding colors (Plus tier)
5. Share web link or embed on your site
Result: Content repurposed to new format instantly

### Workflow: Client Proposal Creation
1. Prompt: "Create proposal for [service] for [client type]"
2. AI generates problem, solution, pricing structure
3. Add your specific details and case studies
4. Include interactive pricing calculator (embed)
5. Share link instead of static PDF
Result: Professional proposals that track engagement

### Workflow: Course Module Previews
1. Enter your course module topic
2. AI generates overview presentation
3. Add embeds: Loom videos, resources
4. Use as free preview content for marketing
5. Track views with analytics (Pro tier)
Result: Course marketing assets from course content
