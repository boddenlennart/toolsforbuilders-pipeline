## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: LinkedIn Lead Research
1. Install Bardeen Chrome extension
2. Go to LinkedIn search/Sales Navigator
3. Run Bardeen scraper on results
4. Extract names, titles, companies, profiles
5. Export to Google Sheet or CRM
Result: Lead list from LinkedIn searches automatically

### Workflow: Competitive Research Automation
1. Set up workflow to visit competitor sites
2. Scrape pricing pages, feature lists, blog posts
3. Save to Notion or Airtable
4. Run weekly to track changes
5. Get alerts when competitors update
Result: Always know what competitors are doing

### Workflow: Content Curation
1. Browse industry news sites
2. Bardeen extracts headlines and links
3. Saves to your content database
4. Filter and organize for repurposing
5. Never miss relevant content
Result: Content pipeline filled automatically

### Workflow: Form Data to Apps
1. Connect web forms to Bardeen
2. When form submits, Bardeen captures
3. Sends to CRM, email tool, or sheet
4. Triggers follow-up automation
5. All from browser, no backend needed
Result: Form automation without developers
