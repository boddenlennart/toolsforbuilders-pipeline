## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Quick Talking Head Reel
1. Import your video clip
2. Use "Captions" → "Auto captions" to generate text overlay
3. Choose a caption style that matches your brand
4. Trim any dead air at start/end
5. Add trending audio (optional)
6. Export directly to Instagram/TikTok
Result: Polished Reel in under 10 minutes

### Workflow: B-Roll Heavy Carousel Video
1. Import your voiceover/audio
2. Use CapCut's stock video library for B-roll
3. Cut clips to match audio beats
4. Add smooth transitions (0.3-0.5s cross-dissolve works for most)
5. Add captions for accessibility
6. Export at 1080p
Result: Professional-looking video without filming anything

### Workflow: Batch Caption Templates
1. Create one Reel with your preferred caption style
2. Save the project as a template
3. For new videos: duplicate template, swap video
4. Captions regenerate with same styling
5. Export
Result: Consistent caption style across all content, 5 minutes per video after setup

### Workflow: Trend Audio Integration
1. In CapCut, browse trending audio
2. Import your talking head video
3. Overlay trend audio at low volume (10-20%)
4. Time your cuts to audio beats
5. This helps algorithm pickup without drowning your voice
Result: Algorithm boost from trending audio while maintaining your message
