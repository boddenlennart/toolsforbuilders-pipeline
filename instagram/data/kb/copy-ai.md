## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Ad Copy Variations
1. Select "Facebook Ads" or "Google Ads" template
2. Enter product description and target audience
3. Generate 5+ headline and body variations
4. Test top performers in your ad platform
5. Feed winning copy back for more variations
Result: A/B test-ready ad copy in minutes

### Workflow: Email Sequence Creation
1. Use "Email Sequence" template
2. Enter product, audience, and goal
3. AI generates welcome → nurture → conversion sequence
4. Edit each email for your voice
5. Load into email platform
Result: Complete email sequence without starting blank

### Workflow: Landing Page Copy
1. Select "Landing Page" template
2. Input your offer details
3. Generate headline, subheads, bullets, CTA options
4. Mix and match best elements
5. Test different combinations
Result: Conversion-focused copy for landing pages
