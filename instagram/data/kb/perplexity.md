## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Daily Research Routine (Free Tier)
1. Morning: Use 2 Pro Searches for trend research in your niche
2. Midday: Use 1 Pro Search for competitor analysis
3. Afternoon: Use 2 Pro Searches for content fact-checking
4. Basic searches unlimited — use for quick lookups throughout day
5. Save important findings to Collections
Result: Comprehensive daily research within free limits

### Workflow: Sourced Content Creation
1. Pro Search: "[topic] latest developments 2026"
2. Review the cited sources — these become your references
3. Click through to 2-3 sources for deeper context
4. Draft your content with inline citations
5. Your content now has credibility others lack
Result: Research-backed content in 15 minutes

### Workflow: Competitive Intelligence
1. Pro Search: "[competitor name] recent news announcements"
2. Pro Search: "[competitor name] customer reviews problems"
3. Compile findings into opportunities document
4. Identify gaps you can fill
5. Create content addressing those gaps
Result: Competitive positioning based on real data

### Workflow: Fact-Check Before Publishing
1. For each factual claim in your draft, basic search in Perplexity
2. Verify the claim matches cited sources
3. Update claims that can't be verified
4. Add source citations where helpful
5. Publish with confidence
Result: No embarrassing corrections, builds audience trust
