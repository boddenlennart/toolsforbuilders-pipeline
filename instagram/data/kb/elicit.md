## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Research for Authority Content
1. Enter your topic as a research question
2. Elicit finds relevant academic papers
3. AI extracts key findings from top papers
4. Export citations for your content
5. Reference studies in your content for credibility
Result: Data-backed content that builds authority

### Workflow: Literature Review for Niche
1. Define research question for your niche
2. Elicit compiles relevant studies
3. Filter by recency, relevance, citations
4. AI summarizes findings across papers
5. Identify gaps for your unique angle
Result: Deep niche understanding in hours, not weeks

### Workflow: Fact-Check Claims Before Publishing
1. Find claim you want to verify
2. Search Elicit for supporting/contradicting studies
3. Review abstracts and findings
4. Either cite support or update your claim
5. Link to studies for credibility
Result: Accurate, citable content
