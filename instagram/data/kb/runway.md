## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Product Demo Visualization
1. Upload product image
2. Use image-to-video to add subtle motion
3. Motion brush for specific element animation
4. Generate 5-second hero clip
5. Use in ads and landing pages
Result: Professional product animation without videographer

### Workflow: Social Content B-Roll
1. Generate text-to-video clips for visual variety
2. Example: "cozy coffee shop morning light"
3. Create 3-5 second clips for reels
4. Layer over voiceover content
5. Gives professional feel to talking-head content
Result: B-roll library without stock footage costs

### Workflow: Thumbnail Animation
1. Create static thumbnail/image
2. Upload to Runway
3. Add subtle motion (zoom, parallax, element movement)
4. Use as video intro or animated thumbnail
5. Stands out in feeds
Result: Eye-catching animated graphics

### Budget Tip:
- Use free credits to test workflow
- Standard ($12/mo) = ~125 seconds of video/month
- Plan generations carefully, each second costs
