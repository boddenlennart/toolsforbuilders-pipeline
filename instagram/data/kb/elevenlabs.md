## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Content Voiceover Production
1. Write your script
2. Paste into ElevenLabs
3. Select voice (or use your clone)
4. Adjust settings: stability, clarity
5. Generate and download audio
Result: Professional voiceover in minutes

### Workflow: Voice Clone for Brand
1. Record 5 minutes of your voice (clean audio)
2. Upload to ElevenLabs voice cloning
3. AI creates your voice model
4. Generate any content in your voice
5. Scale content without recording
Result: Your voice at scale

### Workflow: Podcast Intro/Outro
1. Write intro and outro scripts
2. Generate with professional voice or clone
3. Add music in your editor
4. Use consistently across episodes
5. Professional sound without voice talent
Result: Polished podcast branding

### Workflow: Video Narration
1. Write video script
2. Generate voiceover in ElevenLabs
3. Adjust pacing and tone
4. Import to video editor
5. Sync with visuals
Result: YouTube/course content faster
