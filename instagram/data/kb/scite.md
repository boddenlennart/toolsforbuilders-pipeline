## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Citation Quality Check
1. Find paper you want to cite
2. Search in Scite to see how others cite it
3. Check: Do other papers support or contradict it?
4. Avoid citing discredited or controversial papers
5. Choose well-supported sources for your content
Result: Credible citations that hold up to scrutiny

### Workflow: Find Evidence For/Against Claims
1. Enter claim you're researching
2. Scite shows papers and citation context
3. Filter for "supporting" or "contradicting" citations
4. Build evidence base for your argument
5. Acknowledge counterarguments for credibility
Result: Balanced, well-researched content

### Workflow: Validate Source Credibility
1. Find source someone cited
2. Check how it's cited by other researchers
3. High supporting citations = well-regarded
4. Many contradictions = controversial
5. Make informed decisions about what to trust
Result: Better information hygiene
