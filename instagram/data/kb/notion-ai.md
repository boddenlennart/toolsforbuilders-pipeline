## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Content Calendar with AI Assist
1. Create a database for content ideas with properties: Topic, Platform, Status, Draft
2. When adding new ideas, use AI: "Generate 5 content angles for [topic]"
3. AI fills in initial drafts, you refine
4. Track from idea to published in one view
Result: Content planning + drafting in single workspace

### Workflow: Meeting Notes to Action Items
1. Paste raw meeting notes into a page
2. Use AI: "Extract action items with owners and deadlines"
3. AI creates structured list
4. Convert to linked tasks in your project database
Result: Meetings → Tasks in 2 minutes

### Workflow: Knowledge Base Q&A
1. Build your business knowledge base (SOPs, guidelines, past decisions)
2. Use Notion AI Q&A to search across all pages
3. Ask questions like "What's our refund policy?" or "How do we handle X?"
4. AI finds and summarizes relevant information
Result: Instant answers from your own documentation
