## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Commercial-Safe Marketing Images
1. Go to firefly.adobe.com
2. Enter prompt for your marketing asset
3. Generate variations
4. Use confidently in paid ads (trained on licensed content)
5. No copyright concerns
Result: Marketing images safe for commercial use

### Workflow: Photoshop Generative Fill
1. Open image in Photoshop
2. Select area to modify
3. Use Generative Fill with prompt
4. AI generates contextual additions
5. Non-destructive layers for edits
Result: Complex edits without manual work

### Workflow: Extend Images (Generative Expand)
1. Have image with wrong aspect ratio
2. Use Generative Expand
3. AI fills in surrounding content
4. Match any platform requirement
5. One source image → multiple formats
Result: One image serves all platforms

### When to use Firefly vs alternatives:
- Firefly: Commercial safety needed, Adobe workflow
- Midjourney: Highest artistic quality
- Gemini Imagen: Free high volume
- Ideogram: Text in images
