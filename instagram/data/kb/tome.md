## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Brand Story Presentation (Paid)
1. Describe your brand story to Tome AI
2. AI generates scrollable narrative with visuals
3. Each "page" flows into the next like a website
4. Add video embeds for testimonials
5. Share link for immersive experience
Result: Brand story that feels like a mini-website

### Workflow: Case Study Showcase (Paid)
1. Enter case study details: problem, solution, results
2. Tome AI creates visual narrative
3. Add data visualizations and before/after images
4. Include client quotes as pull-out sections
5. Send link to prospects instead of PDF
Result: Case studies that engage better than documents

### Workflow: Free Tier Manual Creation
1. Choose template that fits your content
2. Manually add text, images, embeds
3. Use scrollable format for engaging flow
4. Unlimited sharing of created content
5. Note: No AI assist on free tier
Result: Beautiful presentations, but more manual work
