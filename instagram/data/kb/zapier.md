## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Lead to CRM Automation
1. Connect form tool (Typeform, Google Forms)
2. When form submitted → add to CRM
3. Send welcome email automatically
4. Tag lead based on form responses
5. One automation, always running
Result: Leads captured and sorted automatically

### Workflow: Social Content Backup
1. When you post to Instagram/Twitter
2. Zapier saves post to Google Sheet
3. Archives images to Google Drive
4. Creates searchable content library
5. Never lose posted content
Result: Automatic content archive

### Workflow: Email to Task Creation
1. Star/label important emails
2. Zapier creates task in your task app
3. Includes email subject and link
4. Set due date based on rules
5. Inbox zero, tasks captured
Result: Emails become tasks automatically

### Cost comparison:
- Zapier Pro: $29.99/mo for 750 tasks
- Make Core: $10.59/mo for 10,000 ops
- n8n self-hosted: $5/mo VPS, unlimited
- Zapier is most beginner-friendly but priciest
