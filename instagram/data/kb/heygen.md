## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Faceless Course Content
1. Create custom avatar from 2-minute video of yourself
2. Write script for lesson
3. Avatar delivers content in your voice
4. Generate unlimited lessons (Creator tier)
5. Never film again for course updates
Result: Course content without camera setup

### Workflow: Multilingual Content
1. Upload existing video with your face
2. Select target languages
3. HeyGen translates voice AND lip syncs
4. Video appears natively in new language
5. Reach global audience without re-filming
Result: One video → many languages

### Workflow: Personalized Sales Videos
1. Create template video with merge fields
2. Upload prospect list with names/details
3. HeyGen generates personalized video for each
4. Send in email outreach
5. Track engagement
Result: Personal video at scale

### Workflow: Quick Social Explainers
1. Choose stock avatar or use your own
2. Paste script (under 3 min for free)
3. Generate video
4. Download and post to social
5. Test what resonates before investing more
Result: Video content without production
