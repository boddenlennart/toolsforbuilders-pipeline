## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Research-Backed Content Creation
1. Enter topic as a question: "Does X improve Y?"
2. Consensus shows papers and consensus meter
3. Note: "Yes (strong)" or "Mixed evidence" etc.
4. Read AI summaries of key papers
5. Use findings and citations in your content
Result: Science-backed content in minutes

### Workflow: Debunk or Confirm Claims
1. Find claim circulating in your niche
2. Search Consensus for evidence
3. See what peer-reviewed research says
4. Either support with citations or debunk
5. Position yourself as evidence-based authority
Result: Credibility through verification

### Workflow: Deep Dive Research (3 free/month)
1. Choose your most important research topic
2. Use Deep Search (reviews 50 papers)
3. Get comprehensive evidence summary
4. Extract key citations for your content
5. Use remaining Deep Searches strategically
Result: Thorough research when it matters most
