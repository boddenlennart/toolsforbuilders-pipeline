## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Lead Qualification Agent
1. Connect your lead sources (forms, email)
2. Build AI agent to analyze leads
3. Agent scores leads based on your criteria
4. High-score leads get routed to you
5. Low-score leads get automated nurture
Result: Only talk to qualified leads

### Workflow: Customer Onboarding Agent
1. Create agent triggered by new signups
2. Agent sends personalized onboarding sequence
3. Answers common questions via chat
4. Flags complex issues for human review
5. Tracks onboarding completion
Result: Scalable onboarding without hiring

### Workflow: Research Agent
1. Define research topic and sources
2. Agent crawls and extracts relevant data
3. Synthesizes findings into reports
4. Runs on schedule or on-demand
5. Delivers insights to your inbox
Result: Ongoing research without manual work

### Who this is actually for:
- Technical solopreneurs comfortable with AI concepts
- Agencies needing scalable client workflows
- Not for simple "connect A to B" automation
- Consider Zapier/Make first if you're starting out
