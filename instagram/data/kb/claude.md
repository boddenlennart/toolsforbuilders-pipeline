## Solopreneur workflows (concrete step-by-step)

### Workflow: Consistent Instagram Captions
1. Create a Claude Project with 3-5 of your best-performing captions as examples
2. Add your brand voice guidelines as a document (tone, words to avoid, CTA style)
3. When writing new captions, reference the project: "Write a caption for [topic] in my established voice"
4. Edit for final polish — Claude gets you 80% there, you add the human touch
Result: Captions that sound like you wrote them, created in 2-3 minutes instead of 15

### Workflow: Long-Form Blog to Social Content
1. Paste your entire blog post into Claude (1M context means full article + context)
2. Prompt: "Extract 5 standalone insights from this that would work as carousel hooks"
3. For each hook, ask Claude to expand into a 5-slide carousel structure
4. Copy to Canva, design, schedule
Result: One blog post → 5 carousels in 20 minutes

### Workflow: Research-to-Draft Pipeline (with Perplexity)
1. Use Perplexity to research your topic (get cited sources)
2. Paste Perplexity's output into Claude with: "Synthesize this into a [format] for [audience]"
3. Claude's writing quality turns research into polished content
4. Review, edit, publish
Result: Research + writing in one session, 30-40 minutes for a complete piece

### Workflow: Client Invoicing (Claude Cowork + Intuit)
1. In Claude Cowork, connect your Intuit account
2. Describe the work: "Create an invoice for 10 hours of consulting at $150/hr for [client name]"
3. Claude generates the invoice with payment link
4. Review, send directly to client
Result: Professional invoices in 60 seconds, no switching apps
