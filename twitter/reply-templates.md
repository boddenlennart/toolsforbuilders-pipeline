# Reply Templates — Framework Bank
*Don't copy-paste verbatim. Grab the frame, write the reply in your own words.*
*Target: 2-4 sentences. Substantive. Never sycophantic.*

---

## 1. Exchange/Custody Failure
*When an exchange collapses, halts withdrawals, or gets hacked*

> The pattern is always the same: the promise was credible until it wasn't.
> Not your keys, not your coins isn't paranoia — it's the only definition of ownership that survives contact with reality.

---

## 2. Regulatory Overreach / Government Control
*When a government freezes crypto, passes restrictive legislation, or sanctions wallets*

> This is what financial dependence looks like in practice.
> When access to your own value can be revoked by a third party, you never fully owned it. Bitcoin's design anticipated exactly this.

---

## 3. ETF / Institutional Bitcoin
*When someone celebrates ETF inflows or institutional adoption without questioning custody*

> Exposure isn't ownership. BlackRock holds the keys.
> The institutional wrapper changes who benefits from the price move. It doesn't change who controls the exit.

---

## 4. Bear Market Noise
*When someone declares Bitcoin dead, worthless, or a scam during a downturn*

> The people writing obituaries couldn't explain a private key when the price was at $100k.
> The market is a mirror. It shows you who was engaging with the idea and who was engaging with the number.

---

## 5. FOMO / Bull Market Hype
*When someone is buying purely on price momentum with no understanding*

> Conviction built on price performance collapses with the price.
> If your thesis changes when the chart does, you never had a thesis — you had a trade.

---

## 6. "Bitcoin Is Too Technical / Complicated"
*When someone says self-custody is too hard or Bitcoin is inaccessible*

> The complexity is real but it's not permanent — and it's proportional to the responsibility.
> Owning something that no one can take from you requires more than clicking "buy." That's not a bug.

---

## 7. Self-Sovereignty / Freedom Angle
*When someone posts about financial freedom, escaping the system, or personal sovereignty*

> The only version of financial sovereignty that holds is the one that doesn't require trusting anyone else to maintain it.
> Self-custody is where the idea becomes real. Everything else is exposure with extra steps.

---

## 8. Proof of Work / Effort / Building
*When someone posts about discipline, building, putting in the work*

> The reason proof of work resonates beyond mining: it can't be faked.
> The network validates the output, not the story you tell about it. Most of life rewards the signal. Bitcoin rewards the work.

---

## 9. Philosophy / Meaning Threads
*When a philosopher-type posts about agency, authenticity, ownership, or free will*

> Bitcoin provided the clearest frame I've found for thinking about this — what does it actually mean to own something? Not hold a claim to it, but possess it without anyone's permission?
> The answer changes everything downstream.

---

## 10. Central Bank / Fiat Criticism
*When someone criticizes inflation, monetary policy, or central bank decisions*

> The problem isn't the people running the system. It's a system designed to require trusting people at all.
> Rules enforced by men can be changed by men. That's the fundamental issue Bitcoin solves.

---

## 11. When You Agree Strongly
*When someone nails a point you've been thinking about*

> This is the exact distinction most people miss.
> [Add one sentence extending or sharpening the point they made.]

---

## 12. When You Respectfully Disagree
*When a reputable account says something you think is incomplete or wrong*

> Interesting framing, but I'd push back on one point.
> [State the pushback clearly in 1-2 sentences. No hedging. No "just my opinion."]

## Usage Notes
- Mornings: scan feed, pick 3-5 replies from target accounts

- Never reply to the same account twice in the same day
