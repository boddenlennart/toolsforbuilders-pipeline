## Engagement Rules
- Always add genuine value — no "great thread!" replies
- Be first when possible — early replies get maximum visibility
- One reply per thread — never spam
- Keep replies short (2-4 sentences) — depth goes in your own threads
- Tag only when genuinely referencing someone
- Never reply to political content — brand risk
- **Tier 1 (400K+):** Quote-tweet only, never direct reply (replies land in filtered tab)
- **Tier 2 (50K-400K):** Selective replies only — one exceptional reply beats five average
- **Tier 3 (3K-50K):** PRIMARY engagement zone — reply consistently, this is where relationships are built
