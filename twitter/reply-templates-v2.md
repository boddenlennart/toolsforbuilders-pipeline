> The thing I keep coming back to: if AI compresses margins to near-zero, where does that value actually go?
> 
> Not to workers (fewer needed). Maybe to equity holders. Definitely to whoever owns the hardest assets.
> 
> This is why @CathieDWood's AI deflation thesis makes sense. The inflation hedge narrative was always incomplete. Bitcoin might be the volatility hedge for whatever AI does to the economy.

**Shorter version:**

>

>

**With company tag:**

### AI-3: Human Work / Identity in AI Era
*When someone posts about AI replacing jobs, human purpose, or meaning*

> Been thinking about this a lot. If AI handles most cognitive work, what gives human effort meaning?
> 
> One answer: proof of work that machines can't fake. Not the algorithm — the principle. Showing up when you could delegate. Building when you could generate.

**Shorter version:**
> The question "what do humans do when AI does everything?" is the wrong framing. Better question: what can only humans do? What can only humans prove?
> 
> Bitcoin's proof of work — the concept, not the algorithm — might be more relevant than ever.

## Usage Notes

### General Guidelines

- Pick the framework that fits, then make it your own
- Never copy-paste verbatim — these are starting points
- One reply per thread — don't spam
- If you tag a company, make sure it's genuinely relevant
- Keep replies short (2-4 sentences) — depth goes in your own threads

### AI/Economy Replies

- These are new territory — uncertainty is fine
- Don't pretend to have all the answers
- Connect to Bitcoin principles without being preachy
- Tag companies sparingly (this pillar is mostly ideas)

### Company-Aware Replies

- Max 2 companies per reply
- Don't tag the same company more than 2-3x/week
- Make sure the recommendation is genuine
- Critique is fine — be factual, not mocking

### Genuine Contributor Replies

- These should feel like conversation, not content
- The goal is to be someone worth following, not to get follows
- Add value > get engagement
- Build relationships > build metrics
